Section 3: Managing Cravings and Preventing Relapse

Managing Cravings and Preventing Relapse: Chapter Summary

Gather cravings, the 4D method, trigger planning, and managing lapse and relapse in one place.

1-minute read Article 11 of 11 in this section

In this article

  1. Keep in mind
  2. The main questions

A craving isn't an order. It can appear as a temporary urge, grow stronger, and then fade.

Keep in mind

Noticing the craving, using the urge surfing approach or the 4D method, preparing a plan for triggers in advance, and not letting a possible lapse turn into an "it's all over" thought can all help protect your smoke-free status.

The main questions

  • What happened?
  • Which trigger came into play?
  • What will I do next time?

Key takeaways

The craving passes, the plan gets stronger. The goal isn't to get through every hard moment perfectly; it's to learn how to return to being smoke-free and keep it going.

If a craving has just arrived

The 90-second exercise takes you through what to do while you wait for the wave to pass.
