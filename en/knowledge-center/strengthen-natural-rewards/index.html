Section 2: Making a Smoke-Free Life Permanent

Strengthen your natural rewards again

Rather than building your smoke-free life only around "not smoking," support your reward and motivation system with natural routines from daily life — movement, sleep, and breathing.

2-minute read Article 5 of 9 in this section

In this article

  1. Choose three small areas
  2. No need for a big change
  3. Breathing and relaxation

Rather than building a smoke-free life solely around "not smoking," increasing natural routines that bring enjoyment and relaxation can be a more sustainable approach.

Choose three small areas

Movement, sleep, and enjoyment. For example, a short walk, a more regular sleep schedule, and a smoke-free activity you enjoy.

No need for a big change

The goal isn't to build a flawless life all at once. Small but repeatable behaviors can gradually fill the space smoking left behind.

Breathing and relaxation

Slow breathing and brief relaxation exercises can offer a different option during stressful moments, instead of automatically reaching for a cigarette.

Key takeaways

The more daily and accessible your new rewards are, the easier it can become for smoke-free living to feel like your new normal.
